Who

I'm noa — a full-spectrum developer who thinks systems should be warm environments for people, not cold monuments to engineering.

I treat tooling as part of the thinking process, and keep refining environments, abstractions and workflows until the friction mostly disappears. The systems i make follow that instinct: immutable, declarative, reproducible, and designed with usability in mind.

I've shipped infrastructure serving 800k+ users. Right now I'm exploring agentic tools, developer experience with nix, and formal verification of llm-generated code.
